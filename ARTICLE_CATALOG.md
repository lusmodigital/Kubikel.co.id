# Article Catalog — kubikel.co.id

## Scope

This catalog expands the mixed Indonesian `kubikel` entity evidenced by the repository: office/workstation cubicles, toilet cubicles, and electrical cubicles. It plans neutral education, decision support, safety, lifecycle, and evidence-led commercial support without creating location-swapped pages.

## Domain boundaries

- Same-domain cannibalization is prohibited; each brief owns one primary reader job and an explicit exclusion.
- `Cubicle.co.id`, `Cubicle.id`, `Kubikel.id`, `ToiletCubicle.co.id`, `ToiletPhenolic.co.id`, `Phenolic.id`, and `Partisi.co.id` are independent domains and may cover the same queries.
- `/`, `/layanan`, `/tentang-kami`, and `/kontak-kami` retain commercial/company/contact intent.
- Structural, accessibility, hygiene, electrical, arc/fire, K3, standards, and compliance material requires the evidence and expert gates stated below.
